WHAT TO EXPECT

Thoughtful care, clear next steps.

An ADHD evaluation considers more than whether someone feels distracted. The clinical picture may include developmental history, current symptoms, functional impact, treatment history, sleep, mood, anxiety, and other factors that can affect concentration.

Recommendations are based on the clinical picture, your preferences, treatment goals, and what is appropriate for your individual situation.

This may include

  • Attention and concentration
  • Organization and planning
  • Impulsivity
  • Executive functioning
  • School or work impact
  • Previous diagnosis or treatment

Have questions before you begin?

A consultation is a good place to discuss symptoms, goals, and the right next step.
