WHAT TO EXPECT

Thoughtful care, clear next steps.

Medication management is more than writing a prescription. Visits focus on symptoms, treatment history, benefit, side effects, concerns, and whether the current plan continues to fit.

Recommendations are based on the clinical picture, your preferences, treatment goals, and what is appropriate for your individual situation.

This may include

  • Initial psychiatric evaluation
  • Medication review
  • Treatment planning
  • Medication adjustments when appropriate
  • Side-effect and response monitoring
  • Ongoing follow-up care

Have questions before you begin?

A consultation is a good place to discuss symptoms, goals, and the right next step.
